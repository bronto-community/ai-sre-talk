# Reliability from Manual to Autopilot

"AI SRE" today usually means an LLM bolted onto on-call — reactive troubleshooting with humans nudged in or out of the loop. But reliability spans the whole lifecycle: building resilient software, resource and release management, operations. Borrowing SAE's "levels of driving automation," this talk lays out a ladder from manual toil to full autonomy, so you can see where your team really stands and where AI actually helps (and where it doesn't).
